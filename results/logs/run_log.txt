Sensitivity (shifts at least 90 days away from a whole number of years): 742 shifts, 254 reached the real chi-square, p = (254 + 1) / (742 + 1) = 0.3432
  Median shifted chi-square for the shifts near a whole year (< 90 days away): 305.08; for the far shifts: 289.01
  Interpretation limits:
  - The circular shift keeps both series' own autocorrelation, so it is a fairer null than a
    plain shuffle, but the soil and rain series also share a yearly monsoon cycle. A shift near
    a whole year keeps that cycle aligned, which makes the shifted chi-squares large and the
    p-value conservative (large). So a non-significant permutation p-value means the data cannot
    separate the rain-soil link from shared seasonality in this short 4-year record; it does NOT
    show the variables are unrelated, and a small p-value would not prove causation either.
  - It is exploratory, not a definitive test: only 4 seasonal cycles are available.

A9. ASSOCIATION VS CAUSATION
======================================================================
The test shows rain_cat and soil_cat are statistically associated.
It does not prove that rain alone causes soil to be wetter: season (monsoon),
temperature, humidity, evaporation and soil memory all move together with rain.
